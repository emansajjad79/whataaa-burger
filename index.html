Our Story

Born From a Love of Real Burgers

What started as a tiny grill on a busy street corner is now the city's favourite burger joint. At Whataaa Burger we believe great food is simple: fresh ingredients, bold flavours and a whole lot of love.

Every patty is smashed to order, every bun is toasted golden, and every sauce is made in-house from our secret recipes.
